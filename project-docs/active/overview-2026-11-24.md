# Overview (2026-11-24)

One screen that shows every page of the wall and every session, and goes to any of them. It
replaces the **sessions manager** (`TerminalSessionBrowser`, `SessionsDrawerView`) and **Edit
pages** (`PageOverviewView`, with its Add page pill). Decided with the developer on 2026-11-24;
lines marked _(proposed)_ are the agent's and not yet confirmed.

Designs: <https://claude.ai/artifact/6o5GpqEsRSaps2NKKPgApm>, row "D · One wall: the current
design" and the "Ways in" board. Rows A, B and C there are earlier rounds and are superseded. The
boards use a placeholder dark look; the built screen wears the one look like every other surface.
Landscape is parked until the Appearance and Wallpaper settings pages are redesigned.

## The model this assumes

- Three page kinds: **Home**, **Workspace**, **Assistant**. There is no Display page: a Linux app
  is a pane of a Workspace, split and placed like a terminal pane (the Wayland compositor work).
- A **Workspace** page is what a window is today: one split layout of terminal and Linux app panes.
- The wall is one ordered list. A page of any kind can be created and placed anywhere in it.
- One gesture switches pages everywhere. The single exception is a Linux app pane that takes the
  touches inside it: there the user holds and swipes on the pane's border.
- A **session** is one layout in a Workspace's place on the wall. A place holds one or more
  sessions; the one in front is the page on the wall, the others wait in that Workspace's row in
  Overview, and choosing one swaps it in. A session is never a row of several Workspace pages.

## The screen

Top to bottom:

1. **Title, + and close.** "Overview", a dedicated **+** button and a ✕.
2. **Go to.** One field: "Go to a page or session". Not focused on open, so no keyboard comes up
   until it is tapped.
3. **The wall.** One strip of cards in wall order that scrolls sideways. A card is about 150 × 290
   dp on a phone: larger than a thumbnail, smaller than the page, so two cards and part of a third
   show. Under each card: its kind glyph and name. The card of the page Overview was opened from
   has the accent ring; the strip opens scrolled to it.
4. **Session rows.** Under the wall, one row for each Workspace that has more than one session:
   "Sessions of _name_", a + that adds a session to that Workspace, and that Workspace's other
   sessions as smaller cards (about 112 × 216 dp). Each row scrolls sideways on its own. A
   connector ties the row to its Workspace. A Workspace with one session has no row.

Nothing is pinned to the bottom of the screen. With several session rows the screen scrolls down;
the wall strip stays first.

### Cards

A card is drawn from layout facts, not a screenshot: a Workspace draws its pane tree with a few
lines of each terminal pane's text and a plain block for a Linux app pane; a Home page draws its
widgets and icon cells as blocks; Assistant draws its conversation as blocks. Nothing in Overview
holds a bitmap of a page, so the snapshot store Edit pages keeps while open goes away.

- **Tap** a wall card: Overview closes into that page.
- **Tap** a session card: that session comes to the front of its Workspace's place and Overview
  closes into it; the session that was in front takes its card in the row.
- **Hold** a card: it lifts, and its menu opens until the card is moved.
  - Workspace: **New session**, **Save**, Rename, **Close** (the developer's three, plus Rename).
  - Session card: Save, Rename, Close.
  - Home page: Set as home, Rename, Remove (rows from `PageCardMenuPolicy`).
  - Assistant: Rename, Close.
- **Drag** a held card: a wall card moves to any place on the wall, whatever its kind; a session
  card moves within its row. Removal keeps Edit pages' rule (`PageOverviewPolicy`): something that
  holds anything is hidden behind an Undo notice and really removed when the notice goes.

### The + button

A tap opens a drop-down card under the button with three rows: **Home page**, **Workspace**,
**Assistant**. The new page lands right after the card in focus on the strip, and can then be
dragged anywhere _(proposed landing place)_. The button turns to a ✕ while the card is open; a tap
outside closes it.

A session is not on this card: it is added from a Workspace's hold menu, from a session row's +,
or by the existing key binding. Creating one adds the row under that Workspace if it had none.

### Go to

Typing replaces the wall and rows with one list of matches: pages by name, Workspaces also by the
command in front where the terminal already knows it, and sessions by name. The top match is
highlighted and Enter goes to it. Clearing the field brings the wall back.

## Ways in

| # | Way in | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tap the session number, the status bar's first chip | Where the sessions manager opens today (`TermuxActivity.toggleSessionsPanel`). |
| 2 | The page bar's first glyph | On Home, and when the page switcher key pops the bar up. First, because Overview sits before the first page. |
| 3 | Pinch in on a Home page's free area | Already opens Edit pages (`onHomePinchIn`); it now opens Overview. Home pages only: a pinch on a Workspace sizes the text. |
| 4 | Home press while resting on the home page | A setting in Settings › Home › Gestures, with the other Home gestures (`home_preferences.xml`, category `home_gestures`). Only when the launcher is in front, on the home page, with nothing open over it; a second press closes Overview. |
| 5 | Swipe past the first page of the wall | Overview sits before the first page and follows the finger in. |

The other doors that open Edit pages today open Overview instead: Edit pages in the Home actions
sheet, the Pages link in the Appearance editor, and the Settings door.

### The edge swipe

- It is the one page-switch gesture, continued past the first page, whatever kind that page is. A
  wall that starts with a Workspace needs nothing special: the same swipe that pages between
  Workspaces brings Overview in. Where the first page is a Linux app pane that takes the touches,
  the border hold-and-swipe does it, as for any page switch from that pane.
- It replaces the edge resistance at the first page (`PaneWallPolicy.EDGE_RESISTANCE`); the far
  end of the wall keeps its resistance.
- Overview is not a page of the wall: it has no place in wall order, cannot be set as home, and the
  page bar counts no page for it.
- It settles by the wall's own rule (distance or fling) and springs back short of it. From
  Overview a swipe the other way returns to the first page, whichever way Overview was opened.
- Opened any other way, Overview arrives in place (no slide).

## Ways out

✕, Back, a tap on any card, a swipe back to the first page, a second home press. Back with the
field holding text clears the text first; Back with a menu or the + card open closes that first.

## What each old screen's actions become

| Today | In Overview |
|---|---|
| Sessions manager: tap a session or window | Tap a card |
| + new session, long-press for a named one | Workspace hold menu › New session, or a session row's +; the name through Rename |
| Session ⋯: Clone, Rename, End | Session card's hold menu: Rename, Close. Clone is dropped _(proposed)_ |
| Save workspace | Hold menu › Save |
| Saved tab: add to current, replace, delete | A "Saved" row at the end of the screen, shown only when something is saved _(proposed, not drawn)_ |
| Displays group | Gone with the Display page: a Linux app is a pane of a Workspace |
| Footer: Home | The Home cards on the wall |
| Edit pages: drag a card | Drag a wall card, now for every kind |
| Edit pages: remove, Undo | Hold menu › Remove or Close; same Undo rule |
| Edit pages: Set as home, Rename | Hold menu |
| Pills: Add page, Add terminal, Add assistant | The + card |
| Pill: Add display; card menu: Bind app | Gone with the Display page |
| Edit pages: the live, scaled card of the current page | Gone: cards are drawn from layout facts |

## Not settled

1. **Saved**: Save keeps the held session's layout (its panes and what runs in them, as Save
   workspace does today) _(proposed)_. The "Saved" row is not drawn.
2. **Clone** is dropped above; say if it stays.
3. **Card content at 150 dp**: terminal text lines are proposed; Home cards show blocks, not icons.
   Real icons would read from `LauncherIconStore`, which is bounded already.
4. **Landscape and tablets**: parked.

## Building it

Branch from `v2-redesign` (a change in behaviour). This depends on the three-kind wall (ADR 0015's
any-order list, the Display page's retirement); Overview can ship first on today's wall with the
Terminal's windows shown as Workspace cards, and gain free ordering when the wall does.

- `OverviewModel` (pure): the wall's cards and each Workspace's sessions. One list both the screen
  and Go to read.
- `OverviewDropPolicy` (pure): where a held card may land and what a drop does. It asks
  `PageOverviewPolicy` for the removal rules; that class, `PageCardMenuPolicy` and
  `PageOverviewPages` stay, `PageOverviewAddPolicy` shrinks to the + card's rows.
- `OverviewGoTo` (pure): matching and ordering.
- Card drawing reads layout facts only (pane tree, text rows, widget cells); no view is drawn into
  a card.
- The view joins the pages **window layer** through `WindowLayers`.
- The Go to field shows the keyboard the way the drawer's search does; a path that shows the system
  IME calls `TermuxActivity.onSystemImeRequested()` first.
- Removed once Overview covers them: `SessionsDrawerView`, `TerminalSessionBrowser`,
  `PageOverviewView`, `PageCardView`, `OverviewGeometry`, `OverviewCaptionInk`, their strings and
  help topics. Help's "sessions manager" lines and the Home actions sheet's "Edit pages" become
  "Overview".
- Glossary: add **Overview** and **Workspace** (the page kind); retire "sessions manager" and
  "Edit pages" under _Avoid_.

Order of work: the model and the wall strip with tap-to-go (ways 1 to 4 open it) → the + card →
hold menus and dragging → session rows → Go to → the edge swipe (way 5, the one piece on the
page-change path; read `docs/agents/performance.md` first) → removal of the two old screens.
