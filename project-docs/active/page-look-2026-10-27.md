# The Look of a page (PageLook): design, 2026-10-27

Candidate B of the page architecture review
(https://amal-build.taild9c5dc.ts.net/reports/page-architecture-2026-10-27/), grilled with the
developer on 2026-10-27 under ADR 0015 §6 (proposed). Builds on the page list
(`page-list-2026-10-27.md`); starts after its slice 1. No code yet.

## The module

`look/PageLook`, app side, reads and writes through a narrow key-value adapter over the main
preferences (and an in-memory adapter for tests).

- `PageLook.of(page)` returns an immutable, resolved **snapshot**: every surface × property, the
  page's Look stop (still derived by matching values against the shipped Looks, as
  `SurfaceEditorController.wornStop` does today) and the ground inputs (wallpaper dim, backdrop
  opacity, and on the Terminal page the canvas tint). Snapshots are cached per page id.
- Callers hold snapshots and never read prefs. Deleted from `TermuxAppSharedPreferences`: the 15
  getter pairs (status bar ×4, keyboard ×5, dock tint and grain, app bar opacity, extra keys blur,
  surface material ×2), `getSurfaceBaseValue`, `getSurfaceOverrideValue`, `isSurfaceInheriting`,
  `resolveSurfaceValue`'s half, and `WallHalf`. No half-less or page-less reader survives.
- Writes take a scope: Global, or a page.
- PageLook listens to the preferences for look keys, rebuilds only the snapshots a change touches (a
  Base or wall value touches every page, an `@id` key one page) and emits `changed(pageIds)`. Views
  subscribe and re-dress. A crossing keeps the snapshots it took at begin; an edit made mid-slide
  shows at landing.

## Resolution

page override → the surface's wall-wide value → Base.

- **Base** stays the All surfaces strip (`surface_base_*`).
- The surface axis is unchanged: a surface follows Base or holds a wall-wide value of its own
  (`surface_inherit_<slot>_<property>`, the existing override keys).
- A **page override** is the existing key plus `@<pageId>` (e.g. `dock_glass_grain@3`).

What a page may detach: blur, opacity, grain and tint of the dock, status bar and keyboard; its
own surfaces (the canvas on the Terminal page, Widgets on Home pages); and the wallpaper dim and
backdrop opacity, the ground's inputs (candidate C); and the material and its intensity (amended
2026-10-27 in B1: a Look applied at a page scope sets that page's material, so "Home glass,
terminal Solid" stays possible, and the upgrade carries a differing Workspace material onto the
Terminal page). Wall-wide only: Base, glass tint hue and vibrancy, Fancier Glass,
`widgets_on_glass`, and the shapes (corners, margin, Style, sizes).

## Who wears what

A page's chrome wears its snapshot. Layers (drawer, palette, sheets, help) wear the snapshot of the
stop the wall rests on, or of the arriving page when they open mid-crossing. This removes the leak
where Home's drawer (`AppDrawerController` :1630) and corner tab (`PaneGlass.dressTab` :205, called
from `WidgetPaneFrame` :768) wear the Workspace dock's glass.

## Storage, backup, presets

- Overrides live in the main preferences, so the settings backup (`SettingsBackupIo`, all keys) and
  preset JSON carry them with no new format. The backup also exports and restores the `wall_pages`
  file so ids match after a restore. Overrides whose id is not in the page list are pruned on load;
  `Removed(p)` deletes a page's.
- Shipped Looks and custom presets apply at the chip's scope: Global writes Base and the wall-wide
  surface values, a page writes its overrides. A saved custom preset (`surface_custom_preset`) holds
  the wall look only. Presets no longer store halves; an old custom preset with half keys loads with
  Home's values. `surface_look_home` retires.

## Editor (slice B2)

- Each card carries the **scope chip**, "Global | <page name>", only where the surface is shown on
  two or more pages (the canvas, on the Terminal page alone, has none; Widgets gets one from the
  second Home page). Edits write Global by default.
- Reset clears the level of the current scope: the page's overrides under the page, the surface's
  own value under Global. The dot marks a value that differs from the level above.
- The editor and presets build their cards and enumerations from PageLook's schema (surfaces per
  page kind, their properties, the scopes) instead of enumerating slot × property × half
  (candidate I). Minimal visual footprint on the settings.

## Upgrade (slice B1)

Per key: equal (or absent) half values become the wall value. Where `_home` and `_workspace`
differ, Home's becomes the wall value and the Terminal page (and a Display page migrated from the
display window, slice 3) gets the Workspace's as `@id` overrides. The canvas keys stay wall values.
The Widgets surface starts following Base.

## Slices

- **B1**: the module, snapshots, `@id` storage, pruning, backup of `wall_pages`, the upgrade, and
  every reader moved. Visible change: only the drawer and corner tab fix.
- **B2**: the scope chip, Looks and presets at a scope, Reset and the dot, the editor schema.

Legibility wave 2c (ADR 0014) reads PageLook snapshots and the page's ground, after B1 and C.
